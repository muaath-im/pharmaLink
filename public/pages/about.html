Our History
PharmaLink was founded in 2021 by dedicated healthcare professionals to modernize pharmacy accessibility and eliminate paper waste in medicine delivery.
Outdated legacy systems in traditional community pharmacies mean prescriptions and paperwork are often printed at high volume. PharmaLink was designed from the ground up as a sustainable, digital-first healthcare platform offering seamless online medication ordering and doorstep delivery from the comfort of your home.

Our mission is simple: provide safe, accessible, and eco-friendly pharmaceutical services for everyone.
